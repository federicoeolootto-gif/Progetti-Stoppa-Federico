# University of Bonn · Economic History & Inequality

**Research focus:** historical institutions, slavery and regional inequality in Brazil.

International research coursework using historical / IPUMS-type data and instrumental variables (2SLS) to study a complex empirical question. The academic work required interpreting identification assumptions and quantitative estimates, rather than only producing summary statistics.

**Public status:** methodological overview only. No restricted IPUMS microdata, unreleased manuscripts or coauthored materials are distributed here.
