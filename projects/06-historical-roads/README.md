# Historical Royal Roads · Transport & Population

**Question:** Did improvements to major road connections under the Bourbon Kingdom affect local demographic concentration in Southern Italy?

My Applied Economics master's thesis studies municipal population density with a historical panel, proximity-to-road comparisons, fixed effects and an event-study framework. The work required combining archival population information with historical transport geography and evaluating assumptions behind a causal interpretation.

**Evidence in this portfolio:** a concise methods overview only. The original analysis, map layers, dataset and replication scripts remain outside the repository while a prospective academic follow-up is coordinated. No publication, journal acceptance or externally verified causal estimate is claimed here.
